Services

What we handle, and who handles it

Ocean freight, customs, documentation and haulage sit with the same team, so a query on any one of them reaches someone who knows the file.

Reefer and Temperature-Controlled

Most of our volume moves in 40ft reefers. We book the unit, fix the set point, follow the plug from stuffing to discharge and keep the cold chain intact on seafood, perishables, food and beverage and pharmaceuticals.

Ocean Freight

FCL and LCL from any Indian or Middle East port to any port worldwide. We book with the carriers that serve the trade and issue our own House Bill of Lading.

Customs Clearance

Our CHA desk handles export and import clearance at Cochin and the other ports we work. Documents are checked before filing, so queries are answered at the desk rather than at the gate.

Import Handling and Release

For inbound cargo we file the IGM, follow the vessel, release the Delivery Order and arrange delivery to the consignee. Reefer imports are prioritised so the box moves off the quay and back onto power.

Export Documentation

Shipping bills, HBL and MBL, certificates of origin, health and phytosanitary paperwork, bank and letter of credit sets. We prepare and check the set against the terms of the credit, because a rejected set is a delayed payment.

Haulage and Warehousing

Container trucking and inland haulage to and from the port, with gensets where the reefer leg needs them. We also coordinate warehousing and cargo insurance so the cargo is insured and held under the right conditions before loading.

Credentials

Registered, not affiliated

YML is registered with the Federal Maritime Commission as an NVOCC under Organisation No. 035760 and files US-bound cargo under SCAC code YENT. The company is also registered as a Multimodal Transport Operator, so it contracts for the through movement and issues its own House Bill of Lading rather than handing the customer only the carrier's Master BL. YML is a member of the Millennium Logistics Network, which gives us partner coverage at destination.
