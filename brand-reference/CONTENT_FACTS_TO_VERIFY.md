## How to approve a claim
1. Attach the evidence file or URL in `Claim_Evidence_Tracker.csv`.
2. Record the entity/jurisdiction it applies to, the exact approved wording and the reviewer’s name.
3. Set status to APPROVED with the date. Only then may the website copy use it.
