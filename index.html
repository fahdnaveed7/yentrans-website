Yentrans Maritime Logistics Pvt Ltd, trading as YML, has moved cargo out of Cochin since 2009. We also move dry containers, LCL, breakbulk and project cargo from any Indian or Middle East port.

What we do

Six desks, one file owner

Reefer and Temperature-Controlled

Most of our volume moves in 40ft reefers. We book the unit, fix the set point, follow the plug from stuffing to discharge and keep the cold chain intact on seafood, perishables, food and beverage and pharmaceuticals.

Ocean Freight

FCL and LCL from any Indian or Middle East port to any port worldwide. We book with the carriers that serve the trade and issue our own House Bill of Lading.

Customs Clearance

Our CHA desk handles export and import clearance at Cochin and the other ports we work. Documents are checked before filing, so queries are answered at the desk rather than at the gate.

Import Handling and Release

For inbound cargo we file the IGM, follow the vessel, release the Delivery Order and arrange delivery to the consignee. Reefer imports are prioritised so the box moves off the quay and back onto power.

Export Documentation

Shipping bills, HBL and MBL, certificates of origin, health and phytosanitary paperwork, bank and letter of credit sets. We prepare and check the set against the terms of the credit, because a rejected set is a delayed payment.

Haulage and Warehousing

Container trucking and inland haulage to and from the port, with gensets where the reefer leg needs them. We also coordinate warehousing and cargo insurance so the cargo is insured and held under the right conditions before loading.

Temperature control

A reefer gives you no second chance

A dry container that sits an extra day costs money. A reefer that sits an extra day without power can cost the whole consignment. Frozen shrimp, vannamei and pharmaceuticals arrive either in specification or not at all, and no claim settlement puts the shelf life back.

  • Set point temperature confirmed on the booking and written on the HBL.
  • Pre-trip inspected units requested before every reefer booking is confirmed.
  • Plug-in status followed at terminal, on board and at transhipment.
  • Gensets arranged for inland legs so the unit stays powered.

How we work

Enquiry to delivery

Enquiry and rate

Send the commodity, load port, destination, volume and, for reefers, the set point. We come back with a rate, routing and the carrier options that fit.

Booking and equipment

We book space, order the container and set the temperature on the booking. Trucking and gensets are arranged for the inland leg where the move requires them.

Documentation and clearance

Shipping bill and customs clearance are filed by our own CHA desk. We issue the House Bill of Lading and release the set your bank needs.

Sailing to delivery

We follow the vessel through loading and transhipment. On inbound cargo we file the IGM, release the Delivery Order and confirm delivery to the consignee.

Send us the shipment

Commodity, load port, destination and volume. For reefer cargo, the set point. We come back with a rate, routing and transit time.
