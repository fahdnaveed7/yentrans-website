Services

What we do, and where we do it

Ocean Freight

We book FCL and LCL space out of Cochin, Chennai, Tuticorin, Mangalore, Mumbai, Nhava Sheva, Mundra, Kolkata and Vizag, and issue our own bill of lading. Sailings come with the transit time and the cut-offs attached.

Inland Haulage India

Trailers and containers to your factory gate anywhere in India, with stuffing supervised at the plant or at the container freight station. We schedule the run to meet the vessel cut-off, not the other way round.

Customs Clearance

Export declarations in India are filed by our own staff. At the US port the entry is filed, duty settled and release obtained under the same contract, so the consignee is not chasing a broker they have never dealt with.

Reefer and Temperature Control

Set point, ventilation setting and pre-trip inspection are agreed in writing before loading. Genset cover on the inland leg, and the temperature checked at gate-in, at transhipment and again when the container is released in the US.

US Door Delivery

Once the container is released, we move it off the terminal, book the delivery slot and truck it to the consignee's facility, whether that is a distribution centre with a booking window or a single plant.

Documentation and Insurance

Bills of lading, invoices, certificates of origin and import papers drawn up to match what the buyer's bank and the US authorities will ask for. Cargo insurance is coordinated with insurers on request, and warehousing arranged at either end.

Industries

Where the cargo comes from

Different industries break in different places: temperature, documentation, delivery windows, duty exposure. These are the sectors we load from India most often.

Food and Beverage

Shelf life decides the routing, and the buyer's retail dates decide the sailing, not the freight rate.

Seafood and Aquaculture

Set points, pre-trip reports and US import filings that stand up to inspection at the first port.

Pharmaceuticals and Healthcare

Controlled temperature ranges, unbroken custody records, and no improvised handling when a vessel runs late.

Automotive and Components

Production lines do not wait, so transit reliability matters more than the cheapest available sailing.

Chemicals and Polymers

Correct classification, hazard paperwork and declarations that match the tariff line the US entry will use.

Textiles and Apparel

Season windows, high piece counts and buyers who deduct for a late delivery appointment.

Building Materials

Heavy, dense loads where weight limits, lashing and inland trucking costs decide the landed price.

Agriculture and Commodities

Volumes move against a contract date, so booking discipline and fumigation papers come first.

Industrial Equipment and Machinery

Out-of-gauge and breakbulk pieces need survey, lashing plans and a delivery site that can unload.

Temperature control

The class that punishes mistakes fastest

Everything we ship moves under the same discipline. Reefer work simply catches out a forwarder sooner. A set point entered wrongly, a genset that fails on the highway, a pre-trip inspection nobody read: any of these writes off a container before it reaches the water.

We load reefer containers out of Cochin and other Indian ports as a matter of routine, and we run dry FCL, LCL and breakbulk with the same people, the same documentation and checks. If a forwarder can hold a temperature for a month at sea, general cargo is not the thing that will trip it up.

How we work

Booking to delivery

Rate and booking

We quote the sailing, the equipment and the inland leg together. Once you accept, space is booked and the cut-off dates go to your shipping desk.

Loading and export clearance

Equipment reaches your plant, stuffing is supervised, and the export declaration is filed. The container is gated in ahead of the cut-off and the bill of lading issued.

Ocean leg

The cargo sails on our bill of lading. We watch the transhipment port and call you if a connection slips, instead of waiting for you to ask.

US clearance and delivery

Entry is filed before the vessel berths. The container leaves the terminal on release, and a truck takes it to the consignee's facility at the agreed hour.

Send us the shipment details

Tell us the load, the origin, the delivery address in the United States and when it has to arrive. We will come back with a rate and a sailing, not a brochure.
