Company

Since 2009, out of Kochi

Yentrans Maritime Logistics Pvt Ltd started in Kochi in 2009 as an ocean freight forwarder working the Cochin trade. Kerala exports what spoils: frozen shrimp and vannamei, frozen fish, spices, perishables. Handling that cargo properly meant learning reefers in detail, and reefers became the business.

Today YML is an FMC-registered NVOCC and a Multimodal Transport Operator, issuing its own House Bill of Lading and moving cargo from any Indian port to any port worldwide. Branches in Dubai and Oman extended the same work into Middle East origins, and membership of the Millennium Logistics Network covers the destination side.

The general cargo side grew with it. Cashew kernels, coir products, plywood, spices, rubber, granite tiles, textiles, surgical goods and plastics move in dry containers, LCL, breakbulk and project shipments. The method is the same whatever the cargo: know the box, know the paperwork, and tell the customer where things stand before they have to ask.

Mission

To move temperature-controlled and general cargo from India and the Middle East with the paperwork correct, the cold chain intact and the customer told where the shipment stands.

Vision

To be the NVOCC that Indian and Gulf shippers choose first for reefer cargo, trusted because the box is watched, the documents hold up and the delivery lands on time.

Credentials

Registered, not affiliated

YML is registered with the Federal Maritime Commission as an NVOCC under Organisation No. 035760 and files US-bound cargo under SCAC code YENT. The company is also registered as a Multimodal Transport Operator, so it contracts for the through movement and issues its own House Bill of Lading rather than handing the customer only the carrier's Master BL. YML is a member of the Millennium Logistics Network, which gives us partner coverage at destination.
