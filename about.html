Company

Seventeen years of other people's cargo

Yentrans Maritime Logistics began in Kochi in 2009, in a city that has been loading ships for centuries and holds views on how it should be done. Cochin is still the home port. The Dubai and Oman branches came later and extended the same work into international freight forwarding from the Middle East.

The work has not changed much in shape. Somebody needs a container to get from an inland factory to a consignee in another country, and between those two points sit a customs entry, a carrier's booking desk, a transhipment port and a set of documents that must agree with each other. YML arranges that carriage, clears the cargo, moves it inland and issues the bill of lading the shipment calls for.

What has changed is reach. Membership of the Millennium Logistics Network and long working relationships with the shipping lines and with other NVOCCs mean the far end of a lane is usually someone we can telephone.

Mission

To arrange the movement of our customers' cargo with accurate documentation, honest routing advice and a named person answering the telephone, from Indian and Middle East origins to ports worldwide.

Vision

To be the forwarder that customers in India and the Gulf call first, because we tell them what a lane can bear before they commit cargo to it.
