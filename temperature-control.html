Temperature control

A reefer gives you no second chance

Frozen shrimp, vannamei and pharmaceuticals arrive either in specification or not at all, and no claim settlement puts the shelf life back.

Reefer work sits at the centre, not the edge

A dry container that sits an extra day costs money. A reefer that sits an extra day without power can cost the whole consignment.

That is why reefer work sits at the centre of YML rather than at the edge of it. We confirm the set point in writing, ask for pre-trip inspected units, and track the box through stuffing, gate-in, loading, transhipment and discharge. Status and documents are visible to you in our customer portal, so you are not waiting on a phone call to find out where the cargo is.

  • Set point temperature confirmed on the booking and written on the HBL.
  • Pre-trip inspected units requested before every reefer booking is confirmed.
  • Plug-in status followed at terminal, on board and at transhipment.
  • Gensets arranged for inland legs so the unit stays powered.
