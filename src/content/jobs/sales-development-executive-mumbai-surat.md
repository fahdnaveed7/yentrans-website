Yentrans Maritime Logistics is looking for a Sales Development Executive to build our
account base across Mumbai and Surat. This is a territory role: you will be with
customers, not at a desk.

Freight is won on credibility rather than on price alone. The people who do well at
this understand what they are selling well enough to say no to a lane that will not
work, and customers remember that.

## What you would do

- Identify and approach exporters, importers and trading houses across the territory.
- Qualify enquiries properly: lane, commodity, equipment, volume and realistic timing.
- Work with the Kochi and branch desks to put together routings and rates that hold.
- Build repeat business from first shipments rather than chasing one-off bookings.
- Keep opportunity and account records current, so the desk can plan around them.
- Feed back what the market is telling you about rates, capacity and competitors.

## What we are looking for

- One to three years selling freight forwarding, shipping line or 3PL services.
- Working knowledge of FCL and LCL movements, and of what a quotation actually commits us to.
- Existing relationships with shippers in the Mumbai or Gujarat belt are an advantage.
- Willing to travel across the territory; Surat and Hazira are part of the patch.
- Comfortable being the person a customer calls when a booking changes.

## What this role offers

A territory to build rather than inherit, with direct access to the operations teams
who deliver what you sell. Remuneration structure, travel arrangements and reporting
line are discussed through the recruitment process.
