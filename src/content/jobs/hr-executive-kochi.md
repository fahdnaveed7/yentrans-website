Yentrans Maritime Logistics is looking for an organised, proactive and people-oriented HR Executive to support recruitment, employee administration and the development of consistent HR processes. The position is suited to an early-career professional who wants hands-on exposure to a growing logistics organisation.

## Role responsibilities

- Coordinate vacancy postings, candidate screening, interviews and recruitment follow-ups.
- Support joining formalities, onboarding and employee induction.
- Maintain employee records, personnel files, attendance and leave data accurately.
- Collect and reconcile monthly payroll inputs with the Accounts team; do not independently authorise payroll.
- Prepare standard HR letters and internal communications using approved templates.
- Respond professionally to routine employee queries and refer sensitive matters to management.
- Assist with travel and accommodation requests or allowances when approved by management.
- Coordinate with department heads on recruitment needs and workforce records.
- Support implementation of HR processes, policy documentation and reporting under managerial guidance.
- Maintain confidentiality and appropriate access controls for employee information.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in HR, Business Administration, Commerce or a related area preferred.
- 0–2 years of relevant experience; fresh graduates with internship exposure encouraged.
- Clear written and verbal communication; proficiency with Excel and Word.
- Strong organisation, attention to detail and discretion with confidential information.
- Interest in learning recruitment, employee administration and HR operations.

## What this role offers

Hands-on involvement in building structured HR processes within an established logistics company, with exposure to multiple functions and opportunities to develop responsibilities over time. Specific salary, benefits and policies will be communicated through the approved recruitment process.
