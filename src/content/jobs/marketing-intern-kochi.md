Yentrans Maritime Logistics is looking for a Marketing Intern to work alongside our
commercial team in Ernakulam. This is a working internship: you will produce material
that goes out, not sit in on meetings and take notes.

Logistics marketing rewards people who understand the operation. You will spend time
with the freight desk so that what you write about a reefer booking or a customs entry
is accurate, because customers in this industry notice immediately when it is not.

## What you would work on

- Draft and schedule content for the company website and LinkedIn, reviewed before publication.
- Help maintain service and industry pages as the business adds capability.
- Prepare simple sales material: capability one-pagers, lane summaries, port guides.
- Support campaign tracking — what was sent, what came back, what it led to.
- Keep the brand applied consistently across documents and templates.
- Sit with the operations and documentation teams often enough to describe the work correctly.

## What we are looking for

- Studying towards, or recently finished, a degree in marketing, communications, commerce or a related field.
- Clear written English. This matters more than any other single skill in this role.
- Comfortable with spreadsheets and willing to learn whatever tools the work needs.
- Curious about how trade actually moves. No logistics background is expected.
- Organised enough to finish things without being chased.

## What the internship offers

Exposure to a real commercial function in an established logistics business, with
published work you can show afterwards. Duration, stipend and the possibility of a
permanent role are discussed through the recruitment process.
